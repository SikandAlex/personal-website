# Course Plan — Career Investments

Curated picks across Udacity, Coursera, Udemy, and YouTube to align with the next
career chapter. Goals informing this plan:

- **Robotics** (long-term interest, requires ROS + C++ refresh)
- **Datacenters / ML training infrastructure**
- **DevOps** (currently planning AWS Cloud Practitioner)
- **Get back into ML** (last hardcore ML was Silk Labs, several years ago)
- **Marketing** (driving growth on own products like StackPass + future ones)
- **Ecommerce, recommendation systems, LLM fine-tuning, agentic workflows**

Caveat: Udacity nanodegrees are pricey (~$249/mo or $1K–2K lump sum). Goal is
*picking 2–3*, not stacking eight.

---

## Tier 1 — Do these first (highest leverage)

| Nanodegree | Platform | Why for you |
|---|---|---|
| **Agentic AI** ⭐ | Udacity | Direct hit on "agentic workflows." Multi-agent systems are the hottest hiring keyword in 2026 AI engineering. Will materially shift how you talk about CallSaver in interviews. |
| **Generative AI** ⭐ | Udacity | Direct hit on "LLM fine-tuning." Covers RAG, multimodal, deployment. Fills the gap between "I use OpenAI/Claude APIs" (CallSaver) and "I can train/customize models." |
| **Robotics Software Engineer** | Udacity (~$999) | Direct hit on robotics. ROS + C++ + Gazebo + perception. The best Udacity offering for getting taken seriously in robotics — recruiters look for ROS specifically. |
| **Cloud DevOps Engineer** | Udacity | Direct hit on DevOps. Pair with the planned AWS Cloud Practitioner → then push through Solutions Architect Associate cert (~$150 exam). Do AWS cert OUTSIDE Udacity using Stéphane Maarek's Udemy course + practice exams. |

## Tier 2 — After Tier 1, if appetite remains

| Nanodegree | Platform | Why for you |
|---|---|---|
| **Machine Learning DevOps Engineer** | Udacity | Bridges DevOps + ML. MLflow, W&B, pytest for data, model deployment. Less hot than Agentic/GenAI but very relevant for "ML training infrastructure" positioning. |
| **Data Engineering with AWS** | Udacity | Relevant if leaning into datacenter / data infrastructure roles. Pipelines, warehouses, lakes. Less relevant if target is ML training specifically. |
| **Digital Marketing** | Udacity | Direct hit on marketing. Live-campaign-based, real receipts not theory. Useful for driving growth on own products. |
| **AI Product Manager** | Udacity | If you want to do *less* coding and more strategy — relevant for ML PM track. Skip if you want to stay technical. |

## Tier 3 — Specific gaps Udacity doesn't cover well

| Course | Platform | Why for you |
|---|---|---|
| **Recommender Systems Specialization** (U Minnesota) | Coursera | Direct hit on recsys. Udacity has nothing focused. The canonical online recsys course. Free to audit. |
| **Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, and Control** (Kevin Lynch / Northwestern) | Coursera | Deeper math/theory than Udacity's robotics nanodegree. Pair with Udacity Robotics if you want both engineering AND theory. Free to audit. |
| **Hugging Face NLP Course** | Free direct | LLM fine-tuning specifically — Hugging Face's own course is more practical and current than any paid program. Free. |
| **Karpathy: Neural Networks Zero to Hero** | YouTube (free) | If you want to go back to ML fundamentals from first principles — gold standard. Free. |
| **Google Digital Marketing & E-commerce Professional Certificate** | Coursera | Direct hit on ecommerce + marketing. ~$49/mo, finishable in 2 months. |
| **AWS Solutions Architect Associate** (SAA-C03) | Direct via AWS | Logical next AWS cert after Cloud Practitioner. The single most respected AWS cert for hiring. ~$15 Udemy course (Stéphane Maarek) + $150 exam. |

## Tier 4 — deeplearning.ai (Andrew Ng's school)

The single best value across all platforms for your goals — most short courses
are **free** (or ~$50 on Coursera with cert), 1–4 hours each, and updated more
often than any specialization. Stack 5–8 of these over a few months.

### LLM fine-tuning + post-training
| Course | Length | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Fine-tuning and Reinforcement Learning for LLMs: Intro to Post-Training** ⭐ | ~4hr | Newest (2026). Covers RLHF, reward modeling, PPO, GRPO — the actual stack used to train modern LLMs. |
| **Finetuning Large Language Models** | ~2hr | Original Sharon Zhou fine-tuning course. Practical PEFT/LoRA basics. |
| **Generative AI with Large Language Models** (with AWS) | ~16hr | Co-developed with AWS. Covers training from scratch, fine-tuning, RLHF, deployment. The closest deeplearning.ai has to a "production GenAI" course. |

### Agentic workflows
| Course | Length | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Functions, Tools and Agents with LangChain** | ~1.5hr | OpenAI function calling + LangChain tool integration. Foundational. |
| **AI Agents in LangGraph** | ~1hr | Stateful multi-agent graphs. Pairs perfectly with the Udacity Agentic AI nanodegree. |
| **Multi AI Agent Systems with crewAI** | ~2hr | Different framework, same multi-agent patterns. Worth seeing both. |
| **AI Agentic Design Patterns with AutoGen** | ~1hr | Microsoft's multi-agent framework. Round out exposure to the three main agentic frameworks. |

### MLOps / production
| Course | Length | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Machine Learning Engineering for Production (MLOps) Specialization** ⭐ | ~4 months | The serious MLOps program (4 courses). Robert Crowe (TFX team). Better signal than the Udacity ML DevOps nanodegree for half the price. |
| **Building Systems with the ChatGPT API** | ~1hr | Production patterns for LLM apps. |

### RAG / data
| Course | Length | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Knowledge Graphs for RAG** (Neo4j) | ~1hr | Less common RAG pattern, useful for distinguishing yourself. |
| **Building and Evaluating Advanced RAG** | ~1hr | Eval is the part most engineers skip and where senior signal lives. |

### What to skip on deeplearning.ai
- **Deep Learning Specialization** — foundations you already have from BU
- **Machine Learning Specialization** — same, too foundational
- **TensorFlow Developer Professional Certificate** — TensorFlow is fading; PyTorch is the modern default

## Tier 5 — edX

| Course | Why for you |
|---|---|
| **MIT MicroMasters in Statistics and Data Science** ⭐ | If you want rigorous, MIT-credential ML refresh. ~$1,500 total for 5 courses. Tommi Jaakkola's 6.86x is widely considered the most rigorous online ML course. Big signal on a resume. |
| **Linux Foundation: Introduction to Kubernetes (LFS158x)** | Free. Solid DevOps fundamental. Stack with the AWS certs. |
| **Linux Foundation: Introduction to DevOps and Site Reliability Engineering (LFS162)** | Free. Vendor-neutral DevOps cred from a credible source. |
| **MIT: Underactuated Robotics (6.832x)** | Russ Tedrake's course on robot control — deeper than Udacity's robotics nanodegree. Free to audit. Pair with Udacity Robotics if you want both engineering and control theory. |

### What to skip on edX
- **HarvardX CS50AI** — foundational, you're past this
- **MITx 6.00.x intro programming** — way past this
- Most **IBM Professional Certificates** — corporate-flavored, weaker signal than the alternatives above

## What to skip

- **Udacity Self-Driving Cars** — outdated content, lots of better current resources (Karpathy's old Stanford lectures, MIT 6.S094)
- **Udacity Data Scientist** — too generalist for someone with this AI background
- **Udacity Deep Learning** — already covered in BU MS; skip the redundant fundamentals
- **Udacity Front End / iOS / React** — completely off-trajectory

## Recommended sequence (6–9 months)

1. **AWS Cloud Practitioner** *(already planned)* — knock out in 2–4 weeks
2. **AWS Solutions Architect Associate** — Stéphane Maarek's Udemy course, 4–6 weeks of evening study
3. **deeplearning.ai short courses on agents** (LangChain → LangGraph → crewAI → AutoGen, ~6 hrs total) — cheap, fast preview before committing to the Udacity Agentic AI nanodegree
4. **deeplearning.ai Fine-tuning and RL for LLMs (Intro to Post-Training)** — current state of the art, ~4 hrs
5. **Udacity Agentic AI Nanodegree** OR **deeplearning.ai MLOps Specialization** — pick one based on which feels more leverage at that point (~3 months)
6. **Udacity Robotics Software Engineer** — if still interested by then; this is a bigger commitment and worth being sure
7. **Marketing**: skip the nanodegree, just take the Google Digital Marketing & E-commerce Certificate (cheaper, faster)

That sequence builds DevOps credibility (AWS), then doubles down on the
agentic/LLM frontier (most defensible AI eng skill in 2026), and leaves
optional pivots toward robotics or marketing depending on where appetite goes.
